# lights.txt - light presets (slice 3D-6, docs/NEXT_SLICE_BARROW.md 5.6, 7.4; src/lights.h has the format).
# dir: towards the light; ambient + diffuse * (n . dir) lights the characters and the kit's props, multiplied by
# `colour`; `tint` multiplies what is baked (the VS1 village, the painted ground, markers); `clear` is the
# background (`ground`: the outer ground x tint); fog: PVR table fog (colour, linear start..end in view depth).
# `day` is built in (the look of every earlier slice); the others are tuned with tools/render_frame.py renders.
light dusk    dir -0.80 0.40 0.30  ambient 0.36 diffuse 0.62  colour FFB27A  tint D89A72  clear 3A2A3A
# night: brightness knobs, most effect first - tint (baked ground/props), ambient/diffuse + colour (characters, lit
# props), clear + fog colour (sky and far mist; keep the two equal), fog start/end (push out = less haze).
# Was: ambient 0.35 diffuse 0.55 colour 8C99F2 tint 6C7CB8 clear 0B1022 fog 0B1022 9 26 (too dark on hardware).
light night   dir -0.30 0.80 -0.35 ambient 0.48 diffuse 0.62  colour A8B4FF  tint 94A2D8  clear 1E2640  fog 1E2640 11 30
light dawn    dir 0.70 0.45 0.35   ambient 0.46 diffuse 0.58  colour FFC8A8  tint E8B8A8  clear 5A4858  fog 8A6A78 18 44
light morning dir 0.45 0.80 0.40   ambient 0.56 diffuse 0.50  colour FFF6E6  tint FFF4E4  clear ground
